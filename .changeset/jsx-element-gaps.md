Pixi JSX: `tint` takes any colour Pixi does, CSS strings included, not only numbers; containers take a `mask`. HTML JSX: `<td>` and `<th>` take `colSpan` and `rowSpan`, there is an `<output>` element, `<input>` and `<textarea>` take `autocomplete`, and every element takes `spellcheck`. three.js: `createPointerPicker` takes a `dragThreshold`, past which a press is a drag and its click is not dispatched.
