"audio90": minor

Instruments and sound effects are now one thing, a sound. `createAudio80Sound` and `createAudio90Sound` replace `createAudio80Instrument`, `createAudio80SoundEffect`, `createAudio90Instrument` and `createAudio90SoundEffect`, with the types `Audio80Sound`, `Audio90Sound`, `Audio80SoundOptions` and `Audio90SoundOptions`, and `isAudio80Sound` and `isAudio90Sound`. A sound takes a sound effect's `glideTo`, `lengthMs`, `priority` and `maxVoices` beside its own options, and a song's `instruments` are sounds. A sound effect's `instrument` option is gone: spread one sound's options into another's instead. `play(sound, options?)` takes the play's `note`, `glideTo`, `lengthMs`, `volume` and, on the Audio90, `pan`, and returns the play's id. A `lengthMs` of `Infinity` holds the note until `stop(id)`, which releases that play's note, and nothing else, even if its voice has gone to another sound. `noteOn` and `noteOff` are gone from the chips. On the headless chips, a logged play holds its `sound`, `id` and `options`, and a stop is logged too. `renderSoundEffect` is now `renderSound`, and `findSounds` returns `sounds` in place of `effects`, with every sound a module exports, including a song's instruments.
