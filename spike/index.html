pixi-mvt-plugin spike

Scene-wide onUpdate(deltaMs) and onRefresh() for Pixi containers, guaranteed to run every container before any of its descendants.

What to watch.

probe order reads p1 > p2 > p3 > p4. The chain is attached first and p2 is hooked afterwards, once p3 and p4 are already listed. An append-ordered list, which is what Pixi's own onRender keeps, would call p2 last. Those four containers are also visible = false, and refresh regardless: neither pass is gated on visibility, culling or rendering.

drain rounds climbs because the swarm view creates its child views from inside onRefresh. Those children are refreshed on the same tick rather than showing one frame of constructor state.

rebuilds counts full depth-first walks of the scene and only moves under the rebuild strategy. compactions counts tombstone sweeps and only moves under incremental. Raise the spawn rate, then switch strategy to compare.
